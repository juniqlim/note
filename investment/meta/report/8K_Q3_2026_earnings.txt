Meta Reports Third Quarter 2026 Results
Announces Establishment of New Class C Common Stock
MENLO PARK, Calif. – October 21, 2026 – Meta Platforms, Inc. (Nasdaq: META) today reported financial results for the quarter ended September 30, 2026.
"We hit major milestones this quarter toward delivering personal superintelligence for everyone - including launching Muse and Muse Wonder, our first frontier model," said Mark Zuckerberg, Meta founder and CEO. "Our investments are paying off, the core business is strong, and we're seeing velocity across the board."

Agreement with U.S. State Attorneys General
In August 2026, we announced an agreement with 52 attorneys general to resolve litigation related to teen mental health and social media, which provides for payments of up to approximately $18.59 billion. Third quarter 2026 costs and expenses includes a $9.89 billion charge related to this agreement. Excluding this charge, the third quarter 2026: 
•Income from operations of $12.99 billion would have increased by $9.89 billion to $22.88 billion. 
•Diluted EPS of $3.83 would have increased by $3.57 to $7.40.
•Effective tax rate of 24% would have decreased by 7 percentage points to 17%.
Third Quarter 2026 Operational and Other Financial Highlights
•Family daily active people (DAP) – DAP was 3.64 billion on average for September 2026, an increase of 3% year-over-year.
•Ad impressions – Ad impressions delivered across our Family of Apps increased by 10% year-over-year. 
•Average price per ad – Average price per ad increased by 14% year-over-year.
•Revenue – Revenue was $64.60 billion, an increase of 26% year-over-year on both a reported and constant currency basis.
•Costs and expenses – Total costs and expenses were $51.61 billion, an increase of 68% year-over-year. This includes the litigation charge as discussed above. 
•Capital expenditures – Capital expenditures, including principal payments on finance leases, were $34.91 billion. 
•Capital return program – Dividend and dividend equivalent payments were $1.36 billion.
•Cash, cash equivalents, and marketable securities – Cash, cash equivalents, and marketable securities were $82.45 billion as of September 30, 2026. 
•Cash flow – Cash flow from operating activities was $32.79 billion, and free cash flow was negative $2.12 billion.(1)
•Headcount – Headcount was 66,775 as of September 30, 2026, a decrease of 15% year-over-year.

CFO Outlook Commentary
We expect fourth quarter 2026 total revenue to be in the range of $73-76 billion. Our guidance assumes foreign currency is an approximately 1% headwind to year-over-year total revenue growth, based on current exchange rates.
We are raising our expense outlook to incorporate the $9.9 billion charge related to legal proceedings recognized in the third quarter. We now expect full year 2026 total expenses to be in the range of $175-179 billion.
Excluding the third quarter legal charge, we continue to expect 2026 operating income to be above 2025 operating income.
At a segment level, we now expect Reality Labs losses to decline compared to 2025 to a range of $18-19 billion.
We anticipate 2026 capital expenditures, including principal payments on finance leases, to be in the range of $135-145 billion, narrowed from our prior outlook of $130-145 billion. 
Absent any changes to our tax landscape, we expect our fourth quarter 2026 tax rate to be between 15-17%.
Additionally, we continue to monitor active legal and regulatory matters that could significantly impact our business and financial results. For example, we continue to see scrutiny on youth-related issues in several markets and have a number of youth-related trials scheduled in the coming months in the U.S.
Looking beyond 2026, we are still developing our budget and capacity plans for next year, but we expect to continue investing aggressively to support both our core business and emerging AI initiatives. Our current capacity and what is scheduled to come online gives us the flexibility to allocate resources across model training, core business growth, and the development and support of new products.
Our AI investments are already delivering substantial incremental advertising revenue through stronger engagement, better recommendations, and more efficient monetization. Building on this progress, our MSL investments are creating the technology and infrastructure needed to unlock significant new revenue opportunities and strengthen capabilities across the business. While these investments will take longer to generate returns than those in our core business, the breadth of monetization opportunities and internal efficiencies gives us confidence in their long-term potential.
Looking ahead, although we are not providing a full-year 2027 revenue outlook, the benefits our AI investments are delivering to the core business, combined with emerging opportunities from model advances and product launches, position us to sustain strong overall revenue growth throughout 2027.
Separately, we remain focused on improving Reality Labs profitability and expect losses to narrow next year to around $15 billion as we reallocate investment toward other priority areas, primarily AI.

Establishment of New Class C Common Stock 
The Board of Directors has approved the establishment of a new class of publicly listed, non-voting Class C common stock, with an effective date in January 2027.
The approval by Meta's Board follows the unanimous recommendation of a Special Committee of disinterested and independent directors, which was established by the Board to evaluate the company's capital structure. The Special Committee and the Board determined that creating this new class of stock is advisable, fair, and in the best interests of Meta and its shareholders for a number of reasons, including:
•Creating a New Class of Shares for Capital Raising, Employee Compensation, Potential Acquisitions, and Other General Corporate Purposes – The scale of investment required to compete, sustain growth, expand infrastructure, and pursue new strategic initiatives amidst the AI transformation taking place in the industry is significant. The Special Committee determined this new class of common stock will provide Meta the ability to finance its growth and help the company remain a leader among fierce competition both domestic and international. 
•Continuing to Maximize Long-Term Value for All Shareholders – Since Meta's formation, its management team has prioritized long-term goals and value creation, and its stock price has increased by more than 1,700% since its IPO. By contrast, over the same period, the S&P 500 has increased approximately 495%. The Special Committee wants Meta to continue innovating with an emphasis on long-term growth, which the creation of the Class C stock is intended to help facilitate.
•Ensuring Founder and CEO Mark Zuckerberg Continues to Lead Meta into the Next Era – The Special Committee concluded that Mr. Zuckerberg is best suited to lead Meta into its next era and beyond. They recognized that Meta's track record of significant value creation for its shareholders is in large part due to his strong leadership, unique and creative vision, and exceptional management abilities. The creation of the Class C common stock will continue the founder-led model that has driven the company's success since its inception.
Meta intends to file an amended and restated certificate of incorporation in Delaware to establish the Class C common stock, with an effective date of January 19, 2027. The Board has also declared a one-time stock dividend of one share of Class C common stock for each outstanding share of Class A common stock and Class B common stock, contingent upon, and to be paid after, the effectiveness of the amended and restated certificate of incorporation. 
The record date and payment date of the stock dividend will be determined by the Board at a later date. After the payment date of the stock dividend, we expect the market price for our Class A common stock to reflect the effect of a two-for-one stock split. The stock dividend will not change the voting power of any shareholder.
The amended and restated certificate of incorporation has been approved by shareholder written consent. We have also entered into a Founder Alignment Agreement with Mr. Zuckerberg, which has terms related to his sales of Class C common stock received in the stock dividend, his service at the company, and conditions for potential future stock dividends of Class C common stock. 
Meta's Class A common stock will continue to trade on Nasdaq, and we intend to qualify the Class C common stock for listing on Nasdaq upon its issuance as a result of the stock dividend. More information will be available in our Report on Form 8-K and information statement to be filed with the Securities and Exchange Commission. 
Moelis & Company LLC served as independent financial advisor to the Special Committee and Morgan Stanley & Co. LLC served as financial advisor to Meta.

Non-GAAP Financial Measures 
To supplement our condensed consolidated financial statements, which are prepared and presented in accordance with generally accepted accounting principles in the United States (GAAP), we use the following non-GAAP financial measures: revenue excluding foreign exchange effect, advertising revenue excluding foreign exchange effect, and free cash flow. The presentation of these financial measures is not intended to be considered in isolation or as a substitute for, or superior to, financial information prepared and presented in accordance with GAAP. Investors are cautioned that there are material limitations associated with the use of non-GAAP financial measures as an analytical tool. In addition, these measures may be different from non-GAAP financial measures used by other companies, limiting their usefulness for comparison purposes. We compensate for these limitations by providing specific information regarding the GAAP amounts excluded from these non-GAAP financial measures.
We believe these non-GAAP financial measures provide investors with useful supplemental information about the financial performance of our business, enable comparison of financial results between periods where certain items may vary independent of business performance, and allow for greater transparency with respect to key metrics used by management in operating our business. 
Our non-GAAP financial measures are adjusted for the following items: 
Foreign exchange effect on revenue. To calculate revenue on a constant currency basis, we translate current period revenue using the prior year's monthly exchange rates for our settlement or billing currencies other than the U.S. dollar, which we believe is a useful metric that facilitates comparison to our historical performance.
Purchases of property and equipment; Principal payments on finance leases. We subtract both purchases of property and equipment, and principal payments on finance leases in our calculation of free cash flow because we believe that these two items collectively represent the amount of property and equipment we need to procure to support our business, regardless of whether we procure such property or equipment with a finance lease. We believe that this methodology can provide useful supplemental information to help investors better understand underlying trends in our business. Free cash flow is not intended to represent our residual cash flow available for discretionary expenditures. 
For more information on our non-GAAP financial measures and a reconciliation of GAAP to non-GAAP measures, see the "Reconciliation of GAAP to Non-GAAP Results" table in this press release.
